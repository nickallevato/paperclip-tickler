What the numbers mean

  • Today — items in the queue's Today lane across every org you have not hidden: decide-by today, or a date today or already past. Snoozed items do not count. Same rule as decideLane(), so the badge and the lane cannot disagree.
  • Overdue — the subset whose decide-by date has passed (isDecideOverdue()). Only this turns the badge alarm-coloured.
  • New — attention items created since you last opened Tickler (tickler.lastVisit, which the briefing strip already records). Clears the moment you open the page.
